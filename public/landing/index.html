01

Module 1 · Capture

It asks, but only when you’ve stopped.

A voice agent sits in a side panel while you work. It never interrupts mid-typing or mid-sentence, and only asks about something visible on screen.

  • Two signals must agree: the screen is still for 2.5 s and you’ve been quiet for 1.8 s.
  • Ask less, later: never in the first 20 s, never closer than 45 s, at most 5 live questions per 10 minutes. The rest wait for the debrief.
  • Guardrails first: limit, then exception, then when to stop and ask. One guardrail question per task is guaranteed.

02

Module 2 · Map

A debrief that ends in a teach-back.

When the task ends, the apprentice asks what’s still unclear, then explains the whole process back in its own words until you say: yes, that’s how it works.

  • Every step is linked to its screen moment and to your own words.
  • Quotes are verbatim or they’re dropped. Nothing is paraphrased into your mouth.
  • Done means done: at least three follow-ups the task didn’t answer, and a teach-back you confirm.

03

Module 3 · Teach

It stops the new hire before the wrong save.

The Work Map becomes a voice tutor. It watches the new hire’s own screen, asks them to predict the next decision, and steps in before a guardrail is broken, in the expert’s words.

  • A case the expert never showed: a fresh €7,200 equipment invoice.
  • Checks the screen 4 s after a change, and again on every save.
  • Mastery per step: what you’ve got, and what to practice next.

The Apprentice Test

Five questions a good apprentice must answer.

Numbers below are the limits set in code today. Rehearsal measurements get added after the live run, not before.

  1. 01

    When to askKnowing the expert has paused

    Scribe v2 Realtime says the expert stopped talking, and a 128×72 pixel probe says the screen has been still. Both, or it stays quiet.

    2.5 s · 1.8 sscreen still · voice quiet
  2. 02

    What to askA reason, not a repeat

    Ranked guardrail, then decision, then a missing slot. Anything the screen already shows is never asked.

    ≤ 5 / 10 minlive questions; the rest wait
  3. 03

    When it has understoodProof, not a summary

    The debrief ends only after follow-ups on things not yet explained, and a spoken teach-back the expert confirms. A correction counts as not confirmed.

    ≥ 3 follow-ups+ confirmed teach-back
  4. 04

    Whether the new hire learnedA case they’ve never seen

    Mastery per judgment step: a correct prediction, or a screen check that matched the Work Map. The ERP asks the tutor before it saves.

    4 scheck after a change, and on every save
  5. 05

    TrustOff the record, and redacted

    Pause everything with one button. IBAN, email and phone are replaced before storage or any model call. Screen regions are blacked out, with opt-in OCR masking. Names and addresses on screen are next.

    0 keptwhile off the record

Moonshot · people first, then agents

The next hire might be an agent that stops where the expert would.

The same Work Map exports as instructions an AI agent can load, and serves guardrails over MCP at decision time. Autonomy grows one step at a time, only where the agent’s stops match the expert’s.
